🥔 Gruyère Bacon Scalloped Potatoes

Magnifique 4‑Quart Slow Cooker Edition

Ingredients

Cook Mode

1. Prep
Grease the slow cooker lightly with butter. Combine Gruyère and cheddar, reserving about 1 cup for the top layer.
2. Cook Bacon
Cook bacon until crisp. Reserve 1 Tbsp bacon fat. Save half the bacon for finishing.
3. Make Sauce
Add butter to bacon fat. Whisk in flour and cook 1 minute. Slowly whisk in cream and broth. Add garlic and cook until slightly thickened. Stir in Parmesan, Dijon, nutmeg, thyme, salt, and pepper.
4. Layer
Layer potatoes, sauce, cheese, and bacon in three layers. Finish with reserved cheese on top.
5. Slow Cook
Cook on LOW for 4½–6 hours. Check tenderness beginning at 4½ hours.
6. Finish
Top with reserved bacon and parsley. Rest 10–15 minutes before serving.
